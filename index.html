ECG Clinical Reference Guide

Educational Overview of Common Patterns
Sinus Bradycardia

Heart rate is below the algorithm's configured threshold (typically <60 BPM) while the rhythm remains consistent with sinus rhythm pacing.

Sinus Tachycardia

Heart rate is above the configured threshold (>100 BPM) with a normal sinus rhythm pattern. Frequently physiological.

Atrial Fibrillation

An irregular rhythm pattern with disorganized atrial depolarization and irregular ventricular response detected by a validated ECG algorithm.

Premature Beats (PAC / PVC)

Early ectopic beats that disrupt the underlying cardiac cycle, followed by compensatory intervals.

Conduction Abnormalities

Delays or blocks involving the PR interval (>200 ms) or bundle branch conduction (QRS >120 ms).

ST-Segment Abnormality

Changes in ST segment elevation or depression that warrant immediate clinical evaluation.

QT/QTc Abnormality

QTc outside configured reference limits (>450 ms males, >460 ms females), indicating prolonged ventricular repolarization.

Important: Do not convert these findings into a confirmed disease diagnosis. CardioConnect is an informational telemetry system.
